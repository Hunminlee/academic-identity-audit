## Current/Core

### EMG-based gesture recognition, biosignal learning, and adaptive motor decoding
**ACM classification:** I.5.4 Pattern Recognition — Applications

It is supported by multiple substantive projects with recent activity, including neural decoding with MindForce, upper/lower-limb motor decoding for neural rehabilitation, and active EMG representation-learning work.
