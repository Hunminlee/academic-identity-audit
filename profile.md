# Research Identity Profile

_Last updated: 2026-08-18_

## Evidence and classification rules

This profile governs the 84-message Gmail Promotions audit for June 15 through August 14, 2026. Research status is based on substantive projects or planning records, not isolated keywords, repeated promotional alerts, or name matches. **Current/Core** requires the same underlying area to appear centrally in at least two substantive GitHub or Notion records and to show substantive activity during the 12 months ending August 14, 2026. **Secondary/Adjacent** is genuinely connected to active work but does not meet that recurrence threshold. **Historical** is supported by older completed work with no substantive source activity in that 12-month window.

ACM names and levels follow the [ACM classification reference](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wXjeVwu2G3VWCBFxjyGKhOlKNBYKBSFi/view) and the [ACM Project Classification Guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w8U5XxliTHr9OVME6xKN3QgLHvG8KsSE/view). The guide's rules are applied directly: classify the project's central contribution, use the lowest clearly supported node, and use multiple categories only when both are central.

Targeted Notion searches surfaced the existing research-audit pages but no independent active-project, experiment-plan, or lab-note page. Those pages are therefore used only as cross-checks, not as independent records for satisfying the Current/Core recurrence threshold.

## Gmail audit rules

- An alert naming an actual paper is stronger identity evidence than a message containing only “Hunmin Lee” or “H. Lee,” but a promotional alert is still only a lead, not proof of a citation.
- Exact-title alerts tied to the active generalization and personalization methodology belong in **Academic Signals/Current Research**.
- Exact-title alerts tied to older capacitive/electric-field work, and genuinely useful reading tied to secondary or historical areas, belong in **Academic Signals/Adjacent or Historical**.
- Name-only or initials-only alerts without a matching title, coauthors, venue, DOI, or affiliation belong in **Academic Signals/Needs Review**, including likely-but-unconfirmed namesake cases.
- Generic advertising, irrelevant recommendations, academic-platform upsells, and confirmed off-profile targeting belong in **Promotions/Low Value**.
- Personally targeted outreach from an identifiable individual about the work remains in **Needs Review** unless stronger evidence supports a research-area label.
- Security, account, and terms notices are excluded and left unchanged.
