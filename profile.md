## Current/Core

### Biomedical biosignal gesture recognition, motor decoding, and neural rehabilitation

This work develops learning systems that decode upper- and lower-limb gestures or motor activity from EMG and peripheral neural signals, with rehabilitation and reliable human–machine interaction as central applications.

- **ACM classification:** I.5.4 — Pattern Recognition: Applications; J.3 — Life and Medical Sciences
- **Evidence:** [Cross-motor-decoding](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Cross-motor-decoding) contains upper- and lower-limb decoding experiments and had substantive commits through September 22, 2025; [Neural Computing Interface MindForce](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Neural_Computing_Interface_MindForce) centers peripheral neural-signal gesture decoding and had substantive experimental commits through December 12, 2025; the [publication record](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Hunminlee.github.io/blob/main/publications.html) lists 2026 work on generalized neural rehabilitation and generalizable EMG gesture recognition.

### Generalizable, adaptive, and personalized learning for biosignals

The recurring problem is robustness to session, subject, sensor, and distribution shifts, using few-shot/meta-learning, prototype adaptation, and co-adaptive learning to reduce calibration and preserve performance across users and time.

- **ACM classification:** I.2.6 — Artificial Intelligence: Learning
- **Evidence:** [Cross-motor Adaptation.py](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Cross-motor-decoding/blob/main/Shared/Adaptation.py) implements episodic few-shot meta-adaptation alongside inter-subject analyses; the [MindForce README](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Neural_Computing_Interface_MindForce/blob/main/README.md) makes inter-session generalization its current target and inter-subject generalization an extension; the [publication record](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Hunminlee.github.io/blob/main/publications.html) includes the 2026 papers *Few-Shot Prototype Adaptation for Generalizable Electromyography Gesture Recognition* and *Upper and Lower-Limb Motor Decoding for Adaptive and Generalized Neural Rehabilitation*.

### Federated, prototype, and meta-learning in heterogeneous distributed environments

This methodological line develops federated learning that can personalize and generalize under non-IID data, noisy networks, and heterogeneous clients, increasingly connecting distributed learning with biomedical applications rather than treating generic distributed AI as the whole research identity.

- **ACM classification:** I.2.11 — Artificial Intelligence: Distributed Artificial Intelligence; I.2.6 — Artificial Intelligence: Learning
- **Evidence:** [Federated Prototype Adaptation Learning](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Federated-Prototype-Adaptation-Learning-for-modulation-classification) and [FedPML](https://github.com/Hunminlee/FedPML) are separate substantive prototype/adaptation and prototype/meta-learning projects; the [2026 publication record](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Hunminlee.github.io/blob/main/publications.html) includes FedPML and recent federated-learning work, while the [current research statement](https://github.com/Hunminlee/Hunminlee.github.io/blob/main/index.html) identifies an active distributed-learning collaboration.

- **ACM classification:** I.5.1 — Pattern Recognition: Models; J.3 — Life and Medical Sciences
- **Evidence:** [EMG-based Gesture Classification using Graphs and GNN](https://github.com/Hunminlee/EMG-based-Gesture-Classification-using-Graphs-and-GNN)
