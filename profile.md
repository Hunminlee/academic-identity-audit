# Research profile

This profile is for the May 15 to Aug. 15, 2026 academic-signal audit.

I use ACM categories to make differently named projects easier to compare. I only include a methodological ACM category when that method is itself a central contribution of the work; otherwise I use the application and domain categories that best describe the project.

## Current / Core

### EMG-based gesture recognition and adaptive motor decoding

ACM classification: I.5.4 - Pattern Recognition: Applications; J.3 - Life and Medical Sciences

This is my main current research direction. It focuses on decoding EMG and peripheral neural signals for gesture recognition, motor decoding, and rehabilitation-oriented interfaces, with recurring work on generalization, adaptation, and personalization across users and sessions.

Evidence:
- MindForce neural decoding: https://github.com/Hunminlee/Neural_Computing_Interface_MindForce
- Cross-motor decoding: https://github.com/Hunminlee/Cross-motor-decoding
- Notion - Long-term Neural Decoding / Session Adaptation: https://app.notion.com/p/Long-term-Neural-Decoding-Session-Adaptation-3c177b24e71681e6ae65edeee5c8c4c6
- Notion - Personalized Wearable Biosignal System: https://app.notion.com/p/Personalized-Wearable-Biosignal-System-3c177b24e7168163adbcc18b38491a43
- Notion - Closed-loop Neural Interface for Rehabilitation: https://app.notion.com/p/Closed-loop-Neural-Interface-for-Rehabilitation-3c177b24e71681ada426cefb47a30c30


## Secondary / Adjacent

### Cross-modal biosignal and semantic representation learning

ACM classification: I.2.6 - Artificial Intelligence: Learning

This direction studies how biosignal representations can be connected with language-derived semantic information through prototype, contrastive, and representation-learning methods. It is closely related to my current biosignal work but is not yet supported by the same breadth of recurring project evidence as the Current/Core area.

Evidence:
- Notion - Language-Guided Biosignal Representation: https://app.notion.com/p/Language-Guided-Biosignal-Representation-3c177b24e716811f8186fedca185ba94

## Historical

### Graph-based EMG gesture modeling

ACM classification: I.5.1 - Pattern Recognition: Models; J.3 - Life and Medical Sciences

This was an earlier line of work using graph neural networks to model relationships among EMG channels for interpretable gesture classification. It remains closely related to my current biomedical work, but the underlying project is no longer active in the current research window.

Evidence:
- EMG-based Gesture Classification using Graphs and GNN: https://github.com/Hunminlee/EMG-based-Gesture-Classification-using-Graphs-and-GNN

ACM classification: I.5.4 - Pattern Recognition: Applications; J.3 - Life and Medical Sciences
