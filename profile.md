# Research profile

This profile is for the May 15 to Aug. 15, 2026 academic-signal audit.

I use ACM categories to make differently named projects and publications easier to compare. I include a methodological ACM category only when that method is itself a central contribution of the work; otherwise I use the application and domain categories that best describe the research.

To keep the publication evidence fair, every verified publication record is assigned to a primary research area below. A paper is listed once under the area that best represents its central contribution, even when it also uses methods that appear in another area.

## Current / Core

### EMG-based gesture recognition, neural motor decoding, and adaptive rehabilitation interfaces

ACM classification: I.5.4 - Pattern Recognition: Applications; J.3 - Life and Medical Sciences

This is my main current research direction. It focuses on decoding EMG and peripheral neural signals for gesture recognition, motor decoding, prosthetic or rehabilitation interfaces, and reliable use across users and sessions through generalization, adaptation, and personalization.

## Secondary / Adjacent

### Cross-modal biosignal and semantic representation learning

ACM classification: I.2.6 - Artificial Intelligence: Learning; J.3 - Life and Medical Sciences

This direction studies how biosignal representations can be connected with language-derived semantic information through contrastive, prototype, and representation-learning methods. It is active and closely related to my current biosignal work, but it does not yet have the same recurring project breadth as the Current/Core area.

It remains relevant to newer biomedical personalization work, but I treat the general distributed-learning methodology as Secondary/Adjacent rather than as a separate Current/Core application identity.
